# Hirelix LinkedIn cold-start outreach

Date: 2026-05-24

## Goal

Start a careful first LinkedIn outbound batch for Hirelix, focused on technical recruiters who can give product feedback and may become early paying users.

## Positioning

Hirelix is an early AI sourcing tool for technical recruiters and independent headhunters. It turns a role brief into an evidence-backed technical shortlist with candidate discovery, fit scoring, GitHub or technical signals, and outreach-ready context.

Keep claims truthful:

- Say "I'm building Hirelix" and "early product".
- Ask for feedback or a quick look before asking for a sale.
- Do not claim proven revenue impact, customer count, funding, or hiring outcomes unless we have direct evidence.

## ICP

Primary:

- Independent technical recruiters and headhunters.
- Boutique search firm founders or partners.
- Startup talent partners who source software engineers directly.

Initial regions:

- United States
- United Kingdom
- Canada
- Australia
- Singapore
- Hong Kong

Strong fit signals:

- Mentions software engineering, AI, data, infrastructure, platform, backend, full-stack, or startup hiring.
- Works with passive candidates or executive/technical search.
- Founder, partner, principal recruiter, senior recruiter, talent partner, or sourcer.
- Posts about sourcing, recruiting workflows, hiring velocity, candidate quality, or AI recruiting tools.

Avoid for first batch:

- Corporate recruiters at very large enterprises with rigid procurement.
- Agency recruiters focused only on healthcare, finance ops, construction, retail, or non-technical roles.
- People with no recent LinkedIn activity and no visible technical hiring focus.

## LinkedIn search queries

- `technical recruiter founder software engineers`
- `engineering recruiter founder`
- `technical recruiter headhunter software`
- `boutique technical search founder`
- `startup talent partner software engineers`
- `technical sourcer AI startup`
- `recruiter backend engineer`
- `recruiter machine learning engineer`

## Connection note

Use when not already connected:

```text
Hi {firstName}, I'm building Hirelix, an AI sourcing tool for technical recruiters. Would love to connect and learn how you source software engineers today.
```

## First DM

Use after connection or where message is allowed:

```text
Hi {firstName}, thanks for connecting. I'm building Hirelix, an early AI sourcing tool for technical recruiters working on software engineering roles.

It turns a role brief into an evidence-backed shortlist with technical signals and outreach context. I'm speaking with a few recruiters to learn where sourcing still feels slow or noisy.

Would you be open to a quick look or a 10 min feedback chat?
```

## Follow-up

Send 4-6 days later if no reply:

```text
Hi {firstName}, quick follow-up in case this got buried. I'm mainly trying to learn how technical recruiters evaluate passive software candidates today, and whether an evidence-backed shortlist would be useful.

Open to a short feedback chat next week?
```

## Execution guardrails

- Send slowly and manually enough to avoid spam-like behavior.
- Stop if LinkedIn shows warnings, CAPTCHA, rate limits, or account restrictions.
- Record every sent request or message in `docs/growth/linkedin-outreach-2026-05-24.csv`.
- Keep the first batch to 30-50 prospects, with an initial live send batch of 5-10 if LinkedIn friction is low.
- Personalize when a profile has a clear technical niche or recent sourcing post.

