# Hirelix 5 minute demo script

Date: 2026-05-24

Use this when a LinkedIn prospect asks for a quick look or joins a 10 minute feedback call.

## Setup

Open:

```text
https://hirelix.online
```

If already logged in, open the production shortlist:

```text
https://hirelix.online/app/search/8450503e-3f1e-4efb-888d-25a27bc5d992
```

## 0:00 - 0:45 Positioning

```text
Hirelix is built for independent technical recruiters and small search teams who need a credible technical shortlist faster than manual LinkedIn review.

The product is intentionally narrow: paste a real role brief, get ranked real profiles, review fit evidence and risks, then decide whether contact/export actions are worth unlocking.
```

## 0:45 - 1:30 Public page

Show:

- Role brief to ranked shortlist workflow.
- No-credit-card start.
- Free preview before paid actions.
- Pricing for Solo, Pro Annual, and one-time add-ons.

Say:

```text
I do not want recruiters paying before they see whether the shortlist is useful. The free path shows the evidence first; paid actions are contact unlocks, export, and client-ready brief capacity.
```

## 1:30 - 3:30 Production shortlist detail

Open the existing production shortlist:

```text
Senior Backend Engineer
```

Show:

- `Shortlist complete`.
- `3 candidates to reach out to first`.
- `7 more to keep reviewing`.
- `150+ deeply reviewed`.
- Candidate evidence and risk labels.
- `Client-ready shortlist`.
- `Outreach approval queue`.
- LinkedIn copy and email copy actions.

Say:

```text
This is the core product promise: not more candidate volume, but fewer profiles with clearer evidence.

The recruiter still makes the judgment. Hirelix gives the proof line, risks to verify, and outreach context so the first message is easier to write and easier to defend to a client.
```

## 3:30 - 4:30 Billing surface

Open:

```text
https://hirelix.online/app/settings#billing
```

Show:

- Current plan and usage.
- Solo and Pro plans.
- Search Pack and Contact Pack.
- Client-ready brief locked until Pro.

Say:

```text
The pricing is designed around recruiter workflow: start free, upgrade when contact unlocks, export, or client-ready briefs save real time on active roles.
```

## 4:30 - 5:00 Feedback ask

Ask:

```text
If you were using this on one active software engineering role, what would you need to trust the shortlist enough to contact the first candidate?
```

Then ask:

```text
Would you be open to trying the free preview on one real role and telling me whether the shortlist is useful?
```

## Demo guardrails

- Do not claim current customers or revenue.
- Do not promise every candidate has GitHub evidence.
- Do not imply outreach is automatic.
- Keep the conversation focused on recruiter trust, candidate quality, evidence, and workflow savings.
