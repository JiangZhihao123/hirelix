# Hirelix service-led SaaS acquisition

Date: 2026-08-05

## Goal

Use paid technical-sourcing work as the entry point for Hirelix adoption:

```text
posted sourcing need
-> paid outcome delivery
-> client reviews the ranked pool
-> client uses Hirelix for the next role
-> recurring SaaS usage
```

The buyer purchases a credible result, not an explanation of the tooling. Hirelix is the delivery engine, with human review before anything is sent to the client.

## Initial offer

### Technical Sourcing Sprint

For one clearly defined software, AI, data, cloud, security, or infrastructure role:

- confirm the sourcing brief and hard constraints;
- run a targeted profile scan;
- deliver the ranked pool found within the agreed scan budget;
- mark the strongest recommendations only where evidence supports them;
- include fit evidence, risks and unknowns to verify;
- include editable outreach starting points for recommended profiles;
- deliver within 48-72 hours after the brief is confirmed.

Do not promise a hire, a fixed number of qualified candidates, or contact details that have not been verified. Do not pad a weak pool to hit a marketing number.

### Pilot pricing

- Profile hourly rate: USD 45/hour.
- Fixed-price first-role pilot: USD 149-299 depending on role complexity and requested scan depth.
- Do not accept USD 10-30 sourcing jobs unless the scope is genuinely a tiny paid sample.

## Task qualification

Apply when all of these are true:

- the client has a real, defined technical role;
- the requested deliverable includes sourcing, profile research, screening, ranking, or shortlist creation;
- the client accepts a fixed-price or hourly contract with payment protection;
- location, work model, seniority and must-have constraints are available or can be clarified;
- delivery can stop at recruiter-ready evidence rather than requiring placement guarantees.

Skip when any of these are true:

- payment is only made after a successful hire;
- the role requires full-cycle interviewing, offer negotiation, background checks or employer-of-record work;
- the client requests bulk unsolicited candidate outreach;
- the client expects private contact data without a lawful, verified source;
- the budget is incompatible with the required profile volume;
- the client asks to move communication or payment outside the platform before the platform permits it.

### Short screening reply

```text
Before I quote this accurately, could you confirm three things: the location/work model, the non-negotiable skills, and whether you need a sourced shortlist or full-cycle recruiting? I am best suited to evidence-backed technical sourcing and first-pass shortlist review.
```

## SaaS conversion after delivery

1. Deliver the paid result through the originating platform.
2. Ask the client to review the ranked pool and identify one useful profile and one miss.
3. For the next role, invite the client to use a Hirelix workspace with the managed delivery still covered by the platform contract.
4. Measure whether the client creates a second sourcing brief, reviews results, expands candidates, or requests another role.
5. Let the client choose between another managed sourcing engagement and independent self-service Hirelix use.

For Upwork-originated relationships, keep payment for the contracted sourcing work on Upwork. Upwork's public [circumvention guidance](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360052511133-Circumvention-and-why-it-s-against-the-rules) does not explicitly address a client independently buying a separate self-service SaaS product. Do not ask a client in Upwork messages to cancel or move the sourcing contract to Paddle. If the client independently chooses a separate Hirelix subscription, keep the SaaS product and the Upwork sourcing engagement clearly distinct, and seek written Upwork clarification before treating that pattern as formally exempt from its terms.

## Metrics

Track the acquisition chain as:

```text
qualified task
-> proposal submitted
-> client reply
-> paid pilot
-> accepted shortlist
-> second role in Hirelix
-> recurring platform-compliant payment
```

The first decision gate is five qualified proposals. Continue only if at least one becomes a real conversation. After ten qualified proposals with zero conversations, revise the offer or task criteria before sending more.
