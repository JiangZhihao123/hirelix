# AI Engineer - VectorShift

**Company:** VectorShift (YC S23)
**Location:** New York, NY, US
**Type:** Full-time, Onsite
**Salary:** $150K - $250K
**Equity:** 0.10% - 0.30%
**Visa:** US citizen/visa required

## About VectorShift

VectorShift develops AI enterprise workflow automation, converting complex manual processes into fully automated AI systems that scale across global teams. The platform enables end-users to run and modify AI pipelines through a no-code interface, while developers can deploy production-grade AI chains via API and SDK. The company is piloting solutions with several enterprises exceeding $1 billion in revenue.

## Key Responsibilities

- Design, build, and deploy AI-powered features in production, including RAG systems, multi-agent orchestration, and evaluation frameworks
- Own backend services and APIs end-to-end using Python, FastAPI, or Django
- Build data pipelines for model training, evaluation, and continuous improvement
- Collaborate with founders, product, and design teams to ship high-impact features
- Ensure scalability, performance, and security across the platform
- Contribute to engineering best practices and technical decision-making

## Required Qualifications

- 2+ years of professional software engineering experience
- Hands-on experience building and deploying AI/ML applications in production
- Solid understanding of relational databases and data modeling
- Experience with cloud infrastructure, containerization (Docker, Kubernetes), and CI/CD pipelines
- Familiarity with RAG pipelines, LLM orchestration, or AI agent architectures
- Git and collaborative development workflow experience
- Knowledge of LLM evaluation techniques and reliability measurement tools
- Strong problem-solving skills and bias toward shipping

## Nice to Have

- Experience working in or building for large enterprises
- Prior startup experience or comfort in high-ambiguity environments

This role demands comfort with ambiguity, fast-paced execution, and shifting priorities based on market feedback. The team seeks exceptionally talented individuals energized by building novel solutions and committed to software craftsmanship.
