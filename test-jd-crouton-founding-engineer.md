## About Crouton

Crouton builds organizational management software for internal teams. The platform helps Ops, Finance, and People functions track personnel allocation across teams and projects, enabling better headcount forecasting and organizational planning.

Founded in 2024 as part of Y Combinator's F24 batch, Crouton addresses organizational visibility challenges. The company operates from New York with a lean team focused on helping enterprises understand resource allocation as clearly as they track financials.

## Role Description

This is an early-stage engineering position with significant ownership. You'll collaborate directly with the founder and customers to build features and shape product direction. The team is small with high trust levels, emphasizing both quality work and work-life balance.
