---
name: follow-ups
description: Manage explicit reminders, recurring role reports and durable working preferences.
---

Distinguish discussion of an event from a delegated future commitment. Resolve timing using the user's request and the supplied timezone. Reuse known details; ask only about ambiguities that affect when or what to execute.

Use one-time reminders or fixed-cadence role reports within their supported scope. Preserve scope and source authorization when modifying an agreement. Pausing or cancelling must affect future execution. Reports return to their original conversation; a reporting agreement does not authorize automatic external sending.

Check actual current schedule state before claiming an agreement is active. There is no implied inbox listener or arbitrary event automation. Report the returned next execution time with its timezone.

Save durable working preferences only when explicitly requested. Candidate/client facts belong in business records, and executable timing belongs in schedules. Preferences can be corrected or forgotten and do not override current instructions or expand authorization.
