---
name: recruiting-records
description: Understand and maintain recruiting facts from JDs, resumes, notes and feedback.
---

Keep original facts, attributed recruiter opinions, model judgments and unknowns distinct. Preserve source files and event dates; a source may contain useful facts without authorizing their storage.

Keep supplied names and identifiers intact. Resolve identities from reliable identifiers and observed evidence. A shared name does not prove that two records are the same person. Preserve an unresolved conflict rather than creating a duplicate or overwriting an existing person to finish a task.

Retain the original JD separately from the current role requirements. Later client feedback can change the current brief; preserve the feedback source, unchanged requirements and earlier history. Outside research or your suggestions do not become client requirements.

A new call or email is a new event. Corrections modify the identified existing event. Reuse the same observed source when an event also changes candidate-role interest or sharing permission. Interest, availability and permission are separate facts; historical permission does not automatically apply to another role or audience.

Respect source date precision and timezone. Planned activity is not completed activity. Attribute opinions as opinions without requiring a full investigation before recording them.

Within a clear internal mandate, save unambiguous facts directly at the user's requested level of detail. Optional missing fields stay unknown. Analysis alone does not authorize changing records. Versions, allowed fields and concrete operations are defined by the tools, not this method.
