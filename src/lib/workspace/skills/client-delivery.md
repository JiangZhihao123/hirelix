# Client delivery

Methods for recommendations, client reports and revisions. Apply the relevant method to the requested deliverable.

## Audience and evidence

Build recommendations from role requirements and supported candidate evidence. Select information appropriate for the recipient and authorized scope, preserving fact-level confidentiality restrictions. Select original CV attachments explicitly when requested. Candidate-role sharing permissions are enforced by the business service.

A recommendation can contain several candidates for one role. Explain the proposed combination and each person's distinct evidence and tradeoffs, keeping internal judgment separate from the client copy. Prepare useful material before presenting a genuine decision to the recruiter. A candidate-specific uncertainty blocks only the affected action; do not hold up other authorized work. Changing the selected candidates requires a new source selection, not merely removing their names from the text while leaving their CVs attached.

Sharing a recommendation link publishes its selected snapshot, not an email or a confirmed delivery. Each person's response is independent. Read recommendation feedback with its candidate, role, source and date; a visitor's submitted name is not verified identity. An interview request is not a scheduled interview. Keep feedback and recruiter decisions as evidence, preserve prior versions, and revise recommendations from current facts. Never treat one client's rejection as a global judgment about the person.

For reports, identify the reporting period and timezone. Attribute activity to its actual event date; preserve undated information as such. Distinguish historical events, current facts, planned activity and unresolved questions.

## Readable recommendation reports

A recommendation report needs scan-friendly structure, not email paragraphs enlarged onto a page. Start with a brief role-specific overview, then a named section per candidate. Use short subheadings for the relevant fit themes, actual Markdown bullets for concrete evidence, and a separate section for material unknowns. Emphasize only key claims or metrics. Keep paragraphs short and avoid repeating the same strengths or unknowns in the summary, evidence and closing. Preserve requested detail; page count is not a reason to squeeze text or remove evidence. Do not invent scores, tables or decorative sections. Email copy remains concise and appropriate to its channel.

## Revision and delivery quality

Evaluate the current saved content against the user's requested purpose, audience and constraints. Preserve source scope and reporting period when revising unless the user changes them. Use the tool's returned content, version and measurements to assess the result; approximate length targets and exact limits are different requirements. Export uses saved content and does not itself revise it.

Deliver an accessible result with material uncertainties. Preparation, review readiness and completed delivery are separate states supported by their respective evidence. A user's report of a completed external delivery can be recorded as such; preparing content does not establish delivery.

Email tools own preview, confirmation and sending. Confirmation applies to the reviewed recipient, content and attachments; changes require the corresponding current preview. An uncertain sending outcome must be reconciled before another send. Preparation or export for the user's own delivery ends at that requested artifact.

## Senior-assistant judgment

Help the recruiter decide by highlighting the material tradeoff, recommending a supported option and completing useful preparation within the delegated scope. Prioritize uncertainties by their impact on the next action. Mention a material opportunity or concern briefly in ordinary prose when useful; no suggestion block or closing decision is required. Match the initiative to the current assignment and existing authorization.

Create or revise explicitly requested internal drafts directly using available evidence and the established audience. Missing optional facts stay marked unknown instead of triggering an intake interview. Ask only about a material conflict or missing requirement that prevents a useful result. Preparation and export for the recruiter do not require a separate approval cycle; external sending and sharing retain their own authorization checks.

## Delivery channels

Prepare one recommendation for the selected role and one or more candidates; frequency and batch size follow the user's request. Default to the recommendation report format so the client page groups each person's reasons, limitations, CV and feedback. A shared page is the presentation; email is a delivery channel. When asked to send a recommendation link, publish the current client copy within the authorized sharing scope, then prepare a short cover email with that link. Selected original CVs may also be attached; a link-only request excludes them. Do not paste the full report into a link email. Traditional full-body emails remain available when requested. The recommendation page offers a ZIP containing the report PDF and selected original CVs for manual delivery. Copying or downloading does not mean sent, and publication alone does not mean the client received it. Gmail is send-only; page feedback is available without inbox access.
