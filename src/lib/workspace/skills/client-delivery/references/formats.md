# Document formats

For format=recommendation, the existing client page associates candidate sections through exact Markdown H2 names: `## Candidate Name` or `## Candidate Name — short description`. Use observed names and include the corresponding structured person_ids. Inside each section, concise H3 themes, short evidence bullets and material unknowns can help readability. Do not repeat the same evidence or uncertainty in every section. Keep internal permission metadata out of client prose. The page keeps per-person feedback separate.

For an internal call brief, lead with the purpose of the conversation, the most important known context and prioritized questions. Do not turn it into a client email. For client email copy, use an appropriate greeting, concise body and closing. A link email normally introduces the recommendation rather than copying its entire report. Respect the requested language, length and structure.

For progress reports, identify period and timezone and separate activity from planned work. Export renders the saved version without rewriting. The existing client recommendation delivery page can provide a ZIP containing the report PDF and selected CVs, subject to its sharing rules; do not invent a package link.
