# Export references

Checked against the linked vendor documentation on 2026-10-28. These are export options, not claims of Hirelix compatibility or market share. A selected list is not a full-account backup. Verify permissions, limits and the actual files supplied; a platform name alone does not prove which fields or relationships are present. Preserve source IDs, status, dates and custom fields as found rather than inventing absent values. “Not documented here” means unknown, not absent.

## Recruit CRM

The account owner on an active paid plan can request **Data Export** (CSV ZIP for candidates, contacts, jobs, companies and deals plus assignments and associations such as notes, meetings, tasks and hotlists) or **Full Export** (the same data plus files attached to candidate, contact and company records, in a separate package). CSV export is limited to once daily and full export to once per 90 days; links expire after three days. The cited documentation does not enumerate every source ID, status, date or custom field and does not establish that every communication is included. [Official account export](https://help.recruitcrm.io/en/articles/3591116-exporting-all-data-for-recruit-crm-account-owners).

List exports are separate and respect selected records, fields and filters; owner/admin or a role with export permission can use them. [Official list export](https://help.recruitcrm.io/en/articles/10773669-exporting-data-to-excel-csv-new-ui). A real trial candidate list and attachment ZIP were inspected locally; they are not a paid full-backup sample, and an older platform-specific importer has since been removed. The sample contained duplicate column names and extensionless PDF files. Match only where the actual contents support it; do not assume every export follows that layout.

## Loxo

An admin can export selected people or companies across pages as CSV. Built-in and custom fields visible on the corresponding list are included; check whether IDs, status and dates appear in the chosen view. Resumes, other attachments, activity-feed history, work and education history are excluded from that CSV; attachments must be downloaded individually. Email Log has a separate export. Job-specific candidate lists and reports are separate; a full data export is requested from support and the vendor describes it as available on contract fulfillment. Source-origin profiles are capped at 500 per rolling 30 days. The exact full-backup relationships, dates, notes and attachments have not been inspected. [Official Loxo export](https://help.loxo.co/en/articles/1238814-export-people-and-company-records-from-loxo).

## Recruiterflow

Advanced Search exports filtered candidates, contacts, companies or jobs to XLSX, subject to role permissions. The exported fields mirror the visible/selected fields and permissions, including visible custom fields; verify IDs, dates, status and links in the actual files. Candidate exports do not include notes, resumes or attachments. The cited guidance does not establish that separate entity lists include all relationships or that a complete account backup is available. [Candidate export](https://help.recruiterflow.com/en/articles/3671789-export-candidate-data-to-excel), [account list exports](https://help.recruiterflow.com/en/articles/8124652-how-to-export-data-from-your-recruiterflow-account).

## Manatal

An admin's table export includes only the rows and columns currently displayed, for candidates, jobs, contacts or organizations; its documented export timestamps are UTC. Admin Data Management → Data Export exports one selected dataset at a time as CSV or XLS with selected fields; the feature is not enabled by default and may require vendor support. A job-specific export includes candidate/application information such as pipeline stage. Check actual IDs, dates and custom fields; the cited dataset documentation does not establish complete note, communication, attachment or cross-entity backup coverage. Prefer CSV over legacy XLS when available. [Table export](https://support.manatal.com/docs/export-function), [dataset export](https://support.manatal.com/docs/export-data), [job candidates](https://support.manatal.com/docs/export-candidates-from-a-job).

## Zoho Recruit

Administrators on paid plans can request account data backups as CSV ZIP; free monthly backup allowances vary by edition and download links expire after seven days. A separate FAQ says attachments can also be exported, and backup logs mention attachment archives, but exact module coverage and association identifiers in an actual backup remain unverified. Notes have a separate CSV export with a documented 3,000-note limit; submission export has an optional notes setting and submissions are included in total backup. Candidate, job and client modules, source IDs, status, dates and custom fields must be checked in the supplied package; the cited backup page does not enumerate their exact fields. [Data backup](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/recruit/essentials/data-management/request-data-backup/articles/request-data-backup), [attachments FAQ](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/recruit/introduction-to-zoho-recruit/articles/how-to-export-and-take-a-backup-of-all-the-data-including-attachments), [notes](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/recruit/essentials/notes/articles/manage-notes), [submissions](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/recruit/module-set-up/sub-modules/articles/submissions).

## Bullhorn ATS/CRM

List CSV exports are entitlement-dependent, with selected visible fields and per-entity limits documented as 5,000–20,000; the field catalog covers candidates, contacts, companies, jobs and submissions, including some IDs, status, dates and custom fields. A candidate list can include only the five most recent submissions when that column is selected. The Notes Activity Report is separately limited to 1,500 results and displays only the first 2,500 characters of the note body. Candidate attachments are downloaded separately; the documented personal-data email/SMS export covers inbound messages only and requires the relevant compliance entitlement. [List export](https://kb.bullhorn.com/ats/Content/BHATS/Topics/exportingDataFromBullhorn.htm), [export fields](https://kb.bullhorn.com/ats/Content/BHATS/Topics/listCustomImportExportFields.htm), [notes report](https://kb.bullhorn.com/ats/Content/BHATS/Topics/generatingNotesActivityReport.htm), [communications](https://kb.bullhorn.com/ats/Content/BHATS/Topics/exportManagement.htm).

An authorized Account/Support contact can request a backup. The vendor describes a Microsoft SQL Server backup containing company records and notes: a standard backup includes only a limited window of attachments, while a final/full backup includes all attachments and is offered on termination or for a fee during a contract. This is not a CSV/XLSX export; Hirelix has not verified direct SQL Server backup restoration or extraction. Exact field and relationship coverage has not been inspected. [Official backup](https://kb.bullhorn.com/ats/Content/BHATS/Topics/requestingDataBackup.htm).

## Other sources

- **LinkedIn Recruiter:** Projects → Pipeline → select profiles → Save to CSV; profile PDFs are another option. Recruiter Lite does not support CSV exports. Selection and monthly download limits apply, and the CSV includes recruiter-entered contact information only. A personal connection archive is not a Recruiter project backup. Applicant XLSX and PDF ZIP are separate exports. [Recruiter export](https://www.linkedin.com/help/recruiter/answer/a412149), [applicant export](https://www.linkedin.com/help/recruiter/answer/a7459505).
- **Crelate:** Contacts → Export can produce Excel for chosen records and columns. Verify whether contacts are candidates or clients and whether notes, attachments, IDs, dates, statuses and relationships are present; the contact list is not proof of a complete account backup. [Official contact export](https://help.crelate.com/en/articles/4120416-exporting-contact-data-to-excel).

Apart from the specific Recruit CRM trial candidate list and attachments described above, no live vendor account or full account backup was inspected in this review. Permission, package contents and reconstruction must be verified with a legitimately supplied export.
