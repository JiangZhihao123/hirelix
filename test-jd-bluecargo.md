Senior/Staff Backend Engineer — BlueCargo (YC S18)

About BlueCargo
BlueCargo is the leading supply chain visibility platform for container logistics. We provide real-time tracking, demurrage & detention management, and AI-powered optimization for trucking companies and freight forwarders moving cargo from marine ports to warehouses. We've raised $15M, have hundreds of daily active users, and are growing fast.

Founded in 2018, we're a 35-person team based in New York and Los Angeles. We're looking for a Senior or Staff Backend Engineer to help us scale our platform to handle millions of container events per day.

What You'll Do
- Design and build scalable backend services that process real-time container tracking data from ports, terminals, and carriers
- Own the architecture of our data ingestion pipeline — we process millions of events daily from dozens of data sources with varying formats and reliability
- Build and maintain our API layer serving our web app, mobile app, and enterprise integrations
- Work on our AI/ML pipeline that predicts container arrival times, detention fees, and optimal routing
- Collaborate with product and data teams to ship features that directly impact customer revenue
- Mentor junior engineers and contribute to engineering culture

What We're Looking For
- 6+ years of backend engineering experience
- Strong proficiency in Python and/or Go
- Deep experience with PostgreSQL, Redis, and message queues (Kafka, RabbitMQ, or similar)
- Experience building and operating data-intensive systems at scale
- Familiarity with cloud infrastructure (AWS preferred — ECS, Lambda, S3, RDS)
- Experience with containerization (Docker, Kubernetes)
- Strong understanding of API design (REST, GraphQL)
- Bonus: experience in logistics, supply chain, or maritime industry
- Bonus: experience with ML/AI pipelines or real-time data processing (Spark, Flink)

Tech Stack
- Backend: Python (FastAPI), Go
- Database: PostgreSQL, Redis, Elasticsearch
- Infrastructure: AWS (ECS, Lambda, S3, RDS), Docker, Kubernetes
